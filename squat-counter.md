A squat is harder to count than a push-up. Bodies are different heights,
phones sit at different distances, and depth is the whole question. FitToll
handles that by measuring you first.

## It calibrates to you

At the start of a session the counter watches you standing and works out
what your normal looks like from where the phone happens to be. Everything
after that is measured against you, at that distance, in that session —
not against a fixed number that assumes a particular body at a particular
range.

The signal it tracks is knee-based and scaled to your own proportions, so
someone tall standing further back and someone short standing closer both
get counted properly.

## Front view or side view

**Front view** counts reps. Stand your phone on the floor facing you,
4–6 feet away, whole body in shot. Keep it low — a camera up on a shelf is
looking down at you and can't see how deep you actually went.

**Side view** counts reps *and* checks your form. Prop the phone at waist
height, 4–6 feet away, so it sees your full body side-on. Depth and forward
lean read much more clearly from the side, which is why form coaching lives
there.

Pick whichever suits the room. Front view is usually easier to set up;
side view tells you more.

## Half-reps don't count

Down and back up, or nothing. A dip that never reaches the bottom is a
movement the counter has seen start and abandon, so the number stays put.
Three strictness levels — Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced — decide how
deep "down" has to be, because your squat and mine are probably not the
same squat.

## Nothing leaves your phone

No video is recorded and nothing is uploaded. Pose detection runs locally
on your device, camera frames are discarded as they're processed, and the
whole thing works offline.

## Using it on its own

**Test Exercise** on the home screen runs a counting session without
blocking anything — useful for getting your phone position right before it
matters.

## Why the count has to be honest

FitToll blocks the apps you lose time to, and reps are how you get in. A
counter you can fool is a blocker you can walk through, so there's no
tapping, no accelerometer, and no skip button.
