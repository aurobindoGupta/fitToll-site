Most blocking apps have a way out, and the way out is usually one tap. A
"skip for now", a five-minute snooze, a settings screen you can reach in
four taps and turn the whole thing off from.

FitToll has none of those. There is no button that dismisses the gate.

## Why a skip button defeats the point

A blocker exists to interrupt a reflex. You reach for the phone without
deciding to, and the block is supposed to put a gap in that motion long
enough for a decision to happen.

A skip button removes the gap. If dismissing the block is one tap and doing
the reps is ninety seconds, the reflex simply learns the tap. After a few
days the blocker has trained you to dismiss it, which is worse than not
having one — you now have the feeling of having done something about the
problem, and none of the effect.

## What FitToll has instead

Three emergency bypasses a day.

They exist for real situations. You need directions and you are on a train.
You need to message someone and there is no floor. Refusing to open in those
moments would just get the app deleted, and an uninstalled blocker blocks
nothing.

So the bypass is always available. It is simply never free:

- **Using one doubles the reps** that app costs on your next unlock.
- **The doubling clears only after one clean unlock** at the higher number.
  You cannot wait it out — you have to pay it back once.
- **Spending all three in a day resets your blocking setup.** Your apps and
  rep counts are gone and you have to choose them again deliberately.

The result is that the escape hatch stays open, and reaching for it costs
enough that the reflex does not learn it.

## What the strictness levels actually control

Beginner, Intermediate and Pro change how much range of movement the counter
demands before it accepts a rep. Pro wants a fuller rep than Beginner does.

They do not change how many reps you owe, and they are not a paywall — all
three are on the free tier, for both exercises.

## The other thing that has no shortcut

The counter reads joint angles rather than motion, so the reps themselves
cannot be faked. Shaking the phone, waving at the camera, or tapping the
screen changes no angle and produces no rep. Half-reps do not count: the rep
lands only after full range, down and back up.

Push-ups add a hip-angle check at the bottom, which is what separates a
push-up from a sag.

This matters for the same reason the missing skip button matters. A gate you
can talk your way past is not a gate.

## And every unlock ends

When the reps are done, the app opens for the unlock window you set. That
window is real time, counted from the moment the app unlocks — closing the app
does not pause it.

That is deliberate. A timer that pauses whenever you leave the app never has
to run out: dip in for a minute at a time and a fifteen-minute unlock can stay
open for days. A fixed window means the gate always comes back.
