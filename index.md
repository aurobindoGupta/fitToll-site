## How the gate works

<div class="steps" markdown="1">

<div class="step" markdown="1">
### Choose
Pick the apps you keep losing time to. Instagram, TikTok, whatever yours are.

### Open
Tap one and FitToll takes the screen before the app does.

Hit the target and the app opens. The timer runs out, it locks again.

## Why it can't be cheated

The counting is the whole product, so it had to be honest.

<div class="facts" markdown="1">

<div class="fact" markdown="1">
#### It reads your joints, not your motion
On-device pose detection tracks real joint angles. Shaking the phone does nothing. Neither does waving at it.
</div>

<div class="fact" markdown="1">
#### Nothing leaves your phone
No video is recorded and nothing is uploaded. The detection runs locally, and it works in airplane mode.
</div>

<div class="fact" markdown="1">
#### The timer counts active use
Earn fifteen minutes and you get fifteen real minutes. Closing the app pauses the clock instead of burning it.
</div>

<div class="fact" markdown="1">
#### One bypass, and it costs you
There's a single emergency bypass. Using it doubles the reps on your next unlock.
</div>

</div>

<p class="strip-foot">Three strictness levels, because your push-up and mine are probably not the same push-up.</p>
